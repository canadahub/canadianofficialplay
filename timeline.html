Timeline

Ancient Era — Indigenous peoples have inhabited the continent for many thousands of years.
1770 — The eastern coast is claimed for Britain by James Cook.
1788 — The First Fleet lands at Warrane (modern-day Sydney Cove).
1851 — The start of the gold rush era.
1901 — The Commonwealth is formed through federation.
1914–18 — The nation participates in the First World War.
1939–45 — The nation is involved in the Second World War.
1967 — A national referendum approves constitutional amendments regarding Indigenous peoples.
1992 — The High Court's Mabo ruling acknowledges native title.
2008 — The government issues a formal National Apology.
2023 — The referendum for a Voice to Parliament is defeated.
